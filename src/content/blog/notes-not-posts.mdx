This site claimed a set of skills for a long time without ever showing the work.
Every explanation of a build decision lived in a chat log, a commit message, or
a thread that scrolled away within a day. None of it was linkable, none of it
was durable, and none of it could be handed to someone evaluating whether I know
what I am doing.

## What goes here

Working notes on developer tools, coding agents, and the systems I ship: Rust
CLIs, agent workflows, and the parts of a Next.js codebase that turn out to be
harder than the documentation suggests. Short when the finding is short. Long
when the reasoning matters more than the conclusion.

## What does not

No engagement bait, no roundups, no framework hot takes. If a note does not
contain something I had to figure out the hard way, it does not get written.

Everything is a file in the repository. Writing a post means adding one file and
committing it. The index, the feed, and the sitemap follow on their own.
